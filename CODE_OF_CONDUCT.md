# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. People who
use assistive technology are welcome here as experts on their own
experience, not as test subjects.

To report a problem, write to hello@accessible-by-default.dev. Reports are
read by Thomas Sweet, the maintainer, and kept confidential.
